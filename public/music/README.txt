CUSTOM BACKGROUND MUSIC GUIDE
============================
The wedding website comes with a built-in algorithmic acoustic chime synthesizer that works in all browsers with zero external audio files.

If you want to use your own song (e.g. romantic Shehnai, Bollywood, flute, or instrumentals):
1. Place your MP3 audio file in this directory:
   e.g. `public/music/wedding-song.mp3`

2. Open `src/js/wedding-config.js` and set:
   audioSrc: '/music/wedding-song.mp3'

3. That's it! When guests click "Tap to open", your MP3 will start playing seamlessly with smooth volume fades.
